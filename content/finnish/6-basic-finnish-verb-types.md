# Finnish verb types
Finnish verbs are categorized into six basic types
## Type 1: Verbs ending in 2 vowels with the last vowel being -a or -ä
This includes verbs that ends in -aa, -ea, -eä, -ia, -iä, -oa, -ua, -yä, -ää, -öä. The *Vartalo* (stem) of these verbs is usually formed by removing the last *-a* or *-ä* from the infinitive form.
